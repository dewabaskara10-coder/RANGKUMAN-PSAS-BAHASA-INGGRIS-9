📜 1. Recount Text & Narrative Text (Folktales)

A. Recount Text

Berfungsi untuk menceritakan kembali pengalaman atau peristiwa masa lalu (*To retell past experiences*[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span)).

  • Sequence Words (Urutan Kejadian): Kata penghubung waktu seperti *First, next, after that, finally*[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
  • Reference Words (Rujukan Kata): Menentukan rujukan kata ganti (*he, she, they, we, it*[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span)). Contoh: *"Rina helped Sinta. **She** was very kind."* (She merujuk pada Rina/Sinta sesuai konteks[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span)).
  • Inference & Numeracy: Menyimpulkan informasi tersirat[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span) dan menjumlahkan angka dalam teks narasi[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span)[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).

B. Narrative Text (Folktales / Cerita Rakyat)

Fokus pada alur cerita fiksi tradisional yang memuat tokoh, masalah (*problem*), penyelesaian (*resolution*), dan pesan moral (*moral value*[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span)).

C. Simple Past Tense (Waktu Lampau)

Wajib menggunakan **Verb 2**[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span) untuk kalimat positif (affirmative).

  • Contoh Perubahan Regular & Irregular: *go → went, see → saw, help → helped*[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span).
  • Hati-hati dengan jebakan soal perbaikan kata kerja salah, misalnya *buyed* (yang benar adalah *bought*[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span)).

🐼 2. Report Text & Simple Present Tense

Berfungsi untuk menyajikan informasi faktual umum mengenai hewan, tumbuhan, fenomena alam, atau benda (*To present general information about animals, plants, or other things*[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span)).

Struktur Teks (Generic Structure)[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span):

  • General Classification: Paragraf awal yang memperkenalkan, mengklasifikasikan, atau mendefinisikan objek yang dibahas[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span). (Contoh: *"Komodo dragons are large reptiles from Indonesia."*[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span)).
  • Description: Paragraf penjelasan rinci mengenai ciri fisik, habitat, makanan, perilaku, kemampuan khusus, hingga ancaman konservasi[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span). (Contoh: *"They have strong legs and sharp claws. They eat meat."*[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span)).

🔢 3. Numeracy in English (Matematika dalam Bahasa Inggris)

Siswa sering diuji melalui soal cerita numerasi berbahasa Inggris[span_26](start_span)[span_26](end_span). Perhatikan kata kunci operasi hitung berikut:

  • ➕ **Penjumlahan (+):** Kata kunci seperti *altogether*, *in total*[span_27](start_span)[span_27](end_span). (Contoh: *"A wildlife team found 24 bird eggs on Monday and 17 on Tuesday. How many eggs did they find altogether?"* $\rightarrow 24 + 17 = 41$[span_28](start_span)[span_28](end_span)).
  • ➖ **Pengurangan / Selisih (-):** Kata kunci seperti *how many more*, *difference*[span_29](start_span)[span_29](end_span). (Contoh: *"A conservation team counted 38 birds in one forest and 52 in another. How many more birds were found in the second forest?"* $\rightarrow 52 - 38 = 14$[span_30](start_span)[span_30](end_span)).
