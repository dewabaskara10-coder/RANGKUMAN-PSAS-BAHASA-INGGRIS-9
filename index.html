📜 1. Recount Text & Narrative Text (Folktales)

Fokus utama: Menceritakan pengalaman masa lalu[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span) atau kisah legenda rakyat dengan struktur alur yang jelas.

Kunci Belajar:

  • Sequence Words: First, next, after that, finally (menunjukkan urutan kejadian)[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).
  • Reference Words: Menentukan rujukan kata ganti (*he, she, they, we, it*)[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span). Contoh: *"Rina helped Sinta. **She** was very kind."* ($\rightarrow$ She merujuk ke subjek yang sesuai konteks)[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).
  • Narrative Moral Value: Pertanyaan seputar pesan moral (*"What can we learn from the story?"*)[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
  • Simple Past Tense: Wajib menggunakan **Verb 2**[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span) untuk kejadian lampau (*go $\rightarrow$ went, see $\rightarrow$ saw, help $\rightarrow$ helped*[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span)). Hati-hati dengan bentuk kata kerja salah seperti *buyed* (yang benar *bought*)[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).

🐼 2. Report Text & Simple Present Tense

Berfungsi untuk menyajikan informasi faktual umum mengenai hewan, tumbuhan, atau benda[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).

Struktur Teks (Generic Structure)[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span):

  • General Classification: Pengenalan & pengelompokan objek di awal teks. Contoh: *"Komodo dragons are large reptiles from Indonesia."*[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span)
  • Description: Penjelasan rinci ciri fisik, habitat, makanan, kebiasaan, hingga ancaman konservasi. Contoh: *"They have strong legs and sharp claws. They eat meat."*[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span)

🔢 3. Numeracy in English

Soal cerita matematika dalam bahasa Inggris[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span). Perhatikan kata kunci operasi hitung:

  • ➕ altogether / in total = Penjumlahan (Contoh: *24 eggs + 17 eggs = 41 eggs altogether*)[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span).
