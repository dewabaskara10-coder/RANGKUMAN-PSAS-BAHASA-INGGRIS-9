📋 Informasi & Struktur Ujian PSAS

Total 35 Soal dalam waktu 90 Menit (20 Pilihan Ganda, 5 PG Kompleks, 5 Benar/Salah, 5 Uraian).

📜 1. Recount Text & Narrative Text (Folktales)

  • Recount Text: Menceritakan pengalaman masa lalu (To retell past experiences). Gunakan kata urutan First, next, after that, finally.
  • Reference (Rujukan Kata): Menentukan rujukan kata ganti seperti he, she, they, we, it sesuai konteks kalimat sebelumnya.
  • Narrative Text (Folktales): Cerita rakyat berfokus pada tokoh, konflik, penyelesaian, dan pesan moral (What can we learn from the story?).
  • Simple Past Tense: Wajib menggunakan Verb 2 (contoh: go → went, see → saw, buy → bought, help → helped).

🐼 2. Report Text & Simple Present Tense

Social Function: Menyajikan informasi umum tentang hewan, tanaman, atau benda.

  • General Classification: Pengenalan/pengelompokan objek (Komodo dragons are large reptiles from Indonesia).
  • Description: Ciri fisik, habitat, makanan, kebiasaan, & ancaman konservasi (They have strong legs and sharp claws).
SubjekAturan VerbContoh Kalimat
I / You / We / They / PluralVerb 1 / have / areOrangutans have long arms. They eat fruit.
He / She / It / SingularVerb 1 + s/es / has / isAn orangutan has long arms. It eats fruit.

🔢 3. Numeracy in English

  • ➕ altogether / in total = Penjumlahan (24 eggs + 17 eggs = 41 eggs altogether).
  • ➖ how many more / difference = Pengurangan/Selisih (52 birds - 38 birds = 14 more birds).
