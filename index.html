🗺️ Cakupan Kompetensi

  • Recount Text: Tujuan teks, urutan peristiwa, kesimpulan pengalaman tokoh, rujukan kata ganti, dan numerasi.
  • Narrative Text (Folktales): Alasan tindakan tokoh, informasi penting cerita, pesan moral, dan rujukan kata ganti.
  • Report Text: General Classification vs Description, ide pokok, ciri fisik hewan, serta tindakan pelestarian/konservasi.
  • Grammar & Numeracy: Simple Past Tense (Verb 2), Simple Present Tense (s/es, is/are, has/have), serta penjumlahan & selisih dalam Bahasa Inggris.

2️⃣ Report Text & Simple Present Tense 🐼

Social Function: Menyajikan informasi umum tentang hewan, tumbuhan, atau benda (To present general information...).

Struktur Teks (Generic Structure):

  • General Classification: Pengenalan umum objek. Contoh: Komodo dragons are large reptiles from Indonesia.
  • Description: Ciri fisik, habitat, makanan, kebiasaan, & ancaman. Contoh: They have strong legs and sharp claws. They eat meat.
SubjectKata Kerja (Verb)Contoh Kalimat
I / You / We / They / PluralVerb 1 / have / areOrangutans have long arms. They eat fruit.
He / She / It / SingularVerb 1 + s/es / has / isAn orangutan has long arms. It eats fruit.

4. Latihan Menulis Report Text: Sun Bears (7-10 Kalimat, 2 Paragraf)

Fakta: Mammals, Tropical forests, Dark fur & strong claws, Eat insects/fruit/honey, Climb trees, Threats: habitat loss & illegal hunting.

Paragraf 1 (General Classification & Appearance):
Sun bears are small mammals that live in tropical forests. They are the smallest members of the bear family. Sun bears have short, dark fur and a distinctive golden patch on their chest. They also have very strong claws and long tongues.

Paragraf 2 (Habits, Diet & Threats):
These animals are excellent climbers and spend much of their time in trees. They eat a variety of foods, including insects, fruit, and honey. Unfortunately, sun bears face serious threats in the wild. Habitat loss and illegal hunting continue to reduce their population.
